How the number is built

Each person has an annual premium from the policy's rate card (a flat amount, an age band, a grade, a per-mille of the sum insured, or one premium for the whole family). The endorsement changes who is covered, so for each person we take the change in annual premium and multiply it by a pro-rata factor for the part of the year it applies.

Joining or increasing: days from the effective date to the policy end, over days in the policy period, both inclusive (daily) or whole months rounded up (monthly). A start on or before the policy start pays in full. Leaving: the part of the year after cover ended is refunded; the leaving day itself is covered. The factor has six decimal places; money is rounded once, at the end.

The total is then rounded by the policy's rule (none, floor, ceiling or nearest, to a unit), tax is charged on the rounded amount, and if the policy has a contribution plan the amount is split between employee and employer, never letting rounding make either side pay more than the whole.
